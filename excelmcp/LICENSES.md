## Risk assessment

### certifi — MPL-2.0 (Weak copyleft) — LOW RISK
MPL-2.0 is a file-level copyleft license. Only modifications to MPL-2.0-licensed *source files* must be released under MPL-2.0. Since `certifi` is consumed as an installed package for TLS certificate verification and we do not modify its source, there is no copyleft obligation. Risk is **LOW** for internal tooling.
